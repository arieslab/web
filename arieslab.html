Institute of Computing · UFBA

Turning code into evidence, and evidence into better software.

The Applied Research in Software Engineering Laboratory (ARIES LAB) conducts quality academic investigations that bring relevant contributions to the literature and to the software industry — from testing and refactoring to the tools practitioners use every day.

Explore our research Meet the group

We are a collaborative research group.

ARIES LAB is a research team at the Institute of Computing at Federal University of Bahia (UFBA), together with the Federal University of Lavras (UFLA) and the State University of Bahia (UNEB).

Funding

Our sponsorships.

ARIES LAB members receive funding from CAPES, FAPESB, CNPq and Tomorrow.

Research lines

From code quality to sustainable software.

Our research interest is related to the following topics:

Empirical Software Engineering

Involves the scientific use of quantitative and qualitative data to understand and improve the software.

Software Testing

Searches related to software testing processes with the aim of improving the quality of the software.

Software Evolution

Area of Software Engineering that investigates the continual development of systems and their changes.

Mining Software Repositories

Analyzes the rich data available in software repositories to uncover interesting facts about software systems, projects, and software engineering.

Sustainable Software Engineering

An emerging discipline with principles, philosophies, and competencies to define, develop, and run sustainable software applications.

People

Science is collective work.

A community of faculty, students and alumni turning questions into knowledge, tools and rigorous software engineering research.
